How It Works

A synchronized AI pipeline simulating the strategic planning process.

01

Idea Analysis Module

The system analyzes the feasibility and uniqueness of a proposed business concept. It identifies target users, value propositions, and potential challenges.

02

Market & Trend Evaluation

Using data-driven techniques, this component assesses market demand, competition, and emerging trends to determine the viability of the idea.

03

Risk & Resource Assessment

Machine learning models estimate potential risks, resource requirements, and operational challenges associated with launching the startup.

04

Strategy Generation

Generative AI produces a structured business strategy covering areas such as product positioning, marketing approach, revenue models, and growth plans.

05

Decision Coordination

A limited number of AI agents organize the analysis and synthesis stages, ensuring a coherent output without requiring complex multi-agent negotiation.
